Reset First-Time Setup?
  1. Connect the device by USB.
  2. Open Logs & Console.
  3. Type NEWMAC and press Enter.
  4. Follow the first-time setup instructions.

Your saved device configuration and servo settings will not be erased.

RadarEye First-Time Setup
  1. Put the Qualia board in upload mode: hold BOOT, press and release RESET while still holding BOOT, then release BOOT.
  2. For a first installation or pairing reset, choose the erase option during installation.
  3. When installation completes, click Next, then press the physical Reset button on the Qualia board.
  4. The console disconnecting after the physical reset is normal. Click Back.
  5. Keep RadarEye selected, click Install Firmware again, reconnect the board, and choose Logs & Console instead of installing again.
  6. Wait for the full device MAC. Ignore a temporary 00:00:00:00:00:00 line during USB reconnection.
  7. Enter the DroidLink Master MAC address when prompted.
  8. Enter an unused Node ID from 2 through 13.

RadarEye saves the pairing information and restarts automatically.
